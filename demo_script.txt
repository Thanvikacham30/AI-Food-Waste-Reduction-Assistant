1-MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT

Hello, my project is the AI Food Waste Reduction Assistant, aligned primarily with SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.

Food can be wasted when people forget what they already have or do not know which ingredients should be used first.

In this prototype, the user adds food items to an inventory. The AI workflow analyzes the inventory, prioritizes items that need attention, retrieves relevant storage guidance, and generates a meal idea using available ingredients.

The project demonstrates RAG through a local sustainability knowledge base and an Agentic AI-style sequence that coordinates inventory analysis, retrieval, and recommendation.

A future version can connect IBM Granite for generative responses and a computer-vision model to identify groceries from photos.

The goal is to help people use what they already have, make better purchasing decisions, and reduce avoidable food waste.
