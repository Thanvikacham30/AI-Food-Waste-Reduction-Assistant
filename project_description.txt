AI-Powered Food Waste Reduction Assistant

Problem:
People often waste food because they forget what they have, purchase duplicates, or do not know which items should be used first.

Solution:
A simple AI-assisted inventory and meal-planning tool that prioritizes food items, suggests meals using existing ingredients, and retrieves storage/waste-reduction guidance.

Primary SDG:
SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production

Additional SDGs:
SDG 2 – Zero Hunger
SDG 13 – Climate Action

AI:
Generative AI, RAG, Agentic AI workflow, recommendation logic, and an optional computer-vision extension.
